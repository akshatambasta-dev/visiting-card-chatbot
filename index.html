The journey

Four decades of hands-on business

It started with our family business, Hindustan Instruments, and it's still moving, now toward a stock market listing (IPO) in 2030.

1968 — Hindustan Instruments

Founded by my family, supplying equipment to BHEL, Grasim, and government departments including Space and Atomic Energy.

1986 — I join, and Computer Components begins

I came into the family business. The same year, my father K. C. Jain set up Computer Components, and I carried it forward through the early days of Indian computing. Hindustan Instruments later expanded into IT and solar.

2009 — The consultancy practice begins

Business owners were tired of theory and needed real answers. I stepped into the CEO role and rebuilt how the business ran: I fixed cash flow, cleared debt, and built a strong consultancy division from the ground up.

2030 — The goal: a stock market listing

We're putting our energy into IT and solar, and building a team of professionals and subject experts to run day-to-day operations. What we are building on is the Buddhi brand, our network and years of goodwill.
